Band-integrated burst fluence and band-restricted isotropic-equivalent
energy. Open circles show measurements that pass the nine-window stability
test; crosses show finite measurements that fail it; gray dashes mark
unavailable band measurements. Blue and orange denote CHIME/FRB and DSA-110,
respectively. Error bars combine statistical uncertainty and the spread under
the tested on-pulse windows, but do not yet include calibration uncertainty.
The lower panel includes only events with an eligible spectroscopic host
redshift; FRB~20230814B, FRB~20230913A, and FRB~20240203A currently use
provisional spectroscopic values from the owner-adopted draft extract. It uses
$E_{\mathrm{iso,band}}=4\pi D_L^2 I_{\mathrm{band}}/(1+z)$ with the Planck18
cosmology. This candidate is not admitted to the manuscript: calibration,
correlated-noise, and owner review remain pending, and seven of 24 band
measurements currently fail or are unavailable.
